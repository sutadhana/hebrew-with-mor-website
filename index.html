About Me

I’m Mor, an Israeli and a native Hebrew speaker and teacher. Since 2022, I have been dedicated to helping adults learn Modern Hebrew.

I have taught more than 2000 lessons to students ranging from complete beginners to advanced learners, each with their own reasons for learning the language.

I enjoy helping students build a solid foundation while gradually becoming more confident and comfortable using Hebrew in real life.

Outside of Hebrew, I also teach Pali and have extensive experience learning and teaching languages.

Teaching Approach

My lessons combine structure with flexibility. I use a structured textbook as a foundation, while also focusing on natural conversation, reading, and understanding how Hebrew is actually used.

I adapt the pace and focus to each student’s needs, whether you want to build a strong foundation, become more confident speaking, develop your reading, or focus on particular areas of Hebrew.

We also use Daily Hebrew, a free practice app I created to review what we cover in our lessons. It keeps track of what you find easy and what needs more practice, so your progress carries from one lesson to the next.

Common Questions

How long is a lesson, and how much does it cost?

Each lesson is 50 minutes and costs $45. You can also purchase a package of 10 lessons and receive a 10% discount. The package is available in the booking section below.

What happens in a lesson?

We’ll focus mainly on conversation and communication, while incorporating grammar, vocabulary, reading, and corrections according to your needs.

Do you teach reading and grammar?

Yes, we’ll build a solid foundation in grammar and vocabulary while helping you develop reading comprehension using modern Hebrew texts.

Can lessons focus mainly on conversation?

Absolutely. Conversation is an important part of my approach, and we can put a strong emphasis on speaking, listening and practical Hebrew for everyday life.

Can I learn Hebrew for living in Israel?

Definitely. I help students prepare for life in Israel by focusing on practical communication, cultural nuances, and the specific Hebrew needed for daily living.

Whom do you teach?

I teach adults of all ages and levels.

Can you teach me to read Biblical Hebrew?

Yes. I can help you learn how to read Biblical Hebrew, even if you are starting from scratch. However, my main focus is Modern Hebrew, and I don’t specialize in Biblical Hebrew, prayer texts, or interpreting the meaning of prayers.
